## Platform

web

## Users

Parents and caregivers of children with autism and developmental needs. They want a clinic they trust — compassionate ABA support, clear credentials, and a team (not a single practitioner) that helps their child build skills for home, school, and community life.

## Product Purpose

TruTalk Therapy provides compassionate, evidence-based behavioural support so children with autism and developmental needs can build meaningful skills, increase independence, and participate more confidently in everyday life. Success means children who communicate, connect, learn, and thrive — and families who feel respected, included, and supported.

## Positioning

A family-centred ABA clinic: individualized programs grounded in Applied Behaviour Analysis, delivered by a clinical team (BCBAs, RBTs, and clinical staff). Distinctive through play-based teaching, positive reinforcement, natural learning opportunities, and close collaboration with families so skills transfer across home, school, and community.

## Operating Context

- Individualized ABA therapy plans (clinic / in-person and family collaboration)
- Communication, social skills, daily living, school readiness, and behaviour support
- Parent and caregiver collaboration as part of treatment
- Multi-therapist clinical team (BCBAs, RBTs, clinical staff)
- Based in Toronto, Canada
- Marketing site for trust-building, booking, and clinic information (courses paused for now)

## Product Principles

1. **Celebrate individuality.** Every child learns differently; therapy is personalized, respectful, and meaningful.
2. **Skills that travel.** What is learned in therapy should transfer to home, school, and community.
3. **Family-centred.** Caregivers are collaborators, not spectators.
4. **Trust through people.** Visible credentials, real faces, and a welcoming environment earn parent confidence.
5. **More than behaviour change.** The aim is communication, connection, learning, and thriving.
